Features

A frictionless maintenance experience.

Every module is designed to eliminate paperwork, reduce errors, and speed up issue resolution.

QR Reporting

Report equipment issues through QR codes in a mobile browser, without installing a native app.

Automated Alerts

The concept includes critical-failure alerts, with messaging integrations under consideration.

Work Order Management

Smart assignment, tool checklists, and digital closure. Everything is traceable.

Inventory Control

Real-time stock deduction and automatic restocking alerts.

Multi-Plant Architecture

Unified portal ideal for contractors managing multiple clients.

Management Reports

Automatic MTTR and availability calculations in one click. Data-driven decisions.

Simple Process

Optimize your maintenance in 3 simple steps.

From reported issue to repaired machine, without paperwork or bottlenecks.

1

Scan and Report

The operator reports the issue at the machine with a simple QR scan from their phone.

2

Assign and Repair

Automatic notification to the right technician with the correct tool and spare part.

3

Measure and Optimize

The plant manager views response-time reports and makes better decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have questions about FixCore?

Answers about the concept and its planned features.

No IoT sensors or specialized hardware are planned. FixCore is designed as a web application accessible from computers and mobile devices.

The proposed pricing model considers licenses for active field technicians. Operators who only report failures would not count as paid technicians. Final terms still need validation.

The platform aims to make failure reporting straightforward on mobile devices. Its ease of use will be evaluated through user testing and feedback.

REST API and webhook integrations are planned capabilities. Their availability and ERP compatibility will be confirmed during development.
